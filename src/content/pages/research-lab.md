## facilities

A 35 m² laboratory with two optical tables and setups for cryogenic and room-temperature experiments. UAM has its own helium liquefier with recovery lines.

![Optics on the lab table with red laser light](/img/lab-red.jpg)

- **Lasers** — Matisse and Tsunami Ti:Sapphire, 2× He-Ne
- **Spectroscopy** — PI 0.5 m + Pixis 1024; Shamrock 750 + iKon-M
- **Detection** — 2 free-space APDs, 4 fibred Thorlabs APDs, 2 fibred MPD APDs, PicoHarp 300, 8-channel qutools correlator
- **Cryogenics** — Janis ST500 He-flow cryostat, attocube XYZ positioners

Techniques: micro-photoluminescence (cryogenic and room temperature); Michelson and Mach-Zehnder interferometry for g⁽¹⁾; Hanbury Brown & Twiss g⁽²⁾ in free space and fibre; Hong-Ou-Mandel two-photon interference; open cavities with nanomotor assemblies.
