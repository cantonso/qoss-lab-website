Experimental quantum optics and quantum materials. PhD (2015) with Prof. L. Viña on III-V exciton-polaritons. Postdoc ([Marie Curie IF 2016–18](https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/702084)) in the group of Prof. P. Senellart (2015–19) on quantum optics with semiconductor quantum dots. Then, in 2019–22, with Prof. C. Schneider in Würzburg and Oldenburg, co-founding the [AG Quantenmaterialien](https://uol.de/en/quantum-materials), on nonlinear and quantum photonics with 2D materials. Since February 2022 he holds an Atracción de Talento contract (Comunidad de Madrid) at UAM, where he develops the QOSS research line within SemicUAM.
