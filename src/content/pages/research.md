We investigate the interaction of light and matter at the quantum level. By leveraging the unique properties of quantum materials, we aim to understand quantum phenomena and exploit them in quantum photonic technologies.

## areas of interest

- **Quantum optics** — photon statistics, bosonic quantum interference, entanglement and superposition, for quantum communication and metrology with photons
- **Quantum materials** — 2D materials and novel compounds with new light–matter interaction properties for quantum photonic technologies
- **Quantum imaging** — entangled light to surpass classical limits in resolution and sensitivity, from biological microscopy to materials characterisation
- **Quantum metrology** — photon-number encoding for high-precision phase measurements and quantum-enhanced sensors
- **Experimental techniques** — spectroscopy, HOM interference, multi-photon correlations and Fabry-Pérot microcavities, at cryogenic and room temperature
