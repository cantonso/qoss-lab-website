## Hackers vs Photon Messengers

On Friday 30 May 2025, Elena del Valle and Carlos Antón gave the public talk _“Hackers vs Photon Messengers: How Quantum Light Protects Information — An Introduction to Quantum Cryptography”_, part of IFIMAC's celebration of the centenary of quantum mechanics and the International Year of Quantum (IYQ2025).

An introduction to how quantum photonics offers new answers to cybersecurity threats. Sala de Grados, Fourier Building, Escuela Politécnica Superior, UAM.

## The QuanTour

![The UAM QuanTour team in the lab](/img/quantour.jpg)

_Adolfo Menéndez Rúa, Raúl Díez Martínez, Carlos Antón-Solanas, Lucía Lázaro Gutiérrez, Diego Marni Sobrino and Alejandro Izquierdo Molina._

We took part in the DPG activity [QuanTour](https://www.quantum2025.de/quantour) for the [International Year of Quantum Science & Technology 2025](https://quantum2025.org), organised by [Doris Reiter](https://cmt.physik.tu-dortmund.de/reiter-group/) and [Tobias Heindel](https://www.tu.berlin/en/ifkp/agheindel): a single-photon source travelling from lab to lab across Europe. Follow its trip on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/quantour.eu/).

**29 July 2024:** the first Hong-Ou-Mandel interference ever measured at UAM, with a path-unbalanced Mach-Zehnder interferometer on the QuanTour sample — by Juan V. Vidal Martínez-Pons, Diego Marni, Alejandro Izquierdo, Lucía Lázaro and Adolfo Menéndez.
